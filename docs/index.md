# Pinchy docs

Pinchy is a desktop AI assistant and agent harness built on Claude Code: a small blue lobster that lives on your screen and does real work.

He needs [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) installed and signed in, runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and has an Android app. He is free and runs on your own Claude plan. [Download the newest version](https://github.com/casualkre/pinchy-releases/releases/latest).

These are the same pages Pinchy looks things up in when you ask him how he works, and the same ones his Help window shows.

## Pages

- [Add-ons: connectors, skills and extensions](https://casualkre.github.io/pinchy-releases/add-ons/) — Three ways to give Pinchy more: connectors link him to the apps and services you use, skills are know-how he loads when a task needs it, and extensions add new looks, tricks, tools and panels.
- [Agents: coworkers with a standing job](https://casualkre.github.io/pinchy-releases/agents/) — An agent is a coworker you give a job to once. It works on its own, on a schedule or when something happens, and writes to you only when it is done, has an update, or needs your help or approval. You do not manage it step by step.
