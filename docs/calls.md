# Calls: Pinchy rings your phone

Pinchy, your agents and krill can ring the Pinchy app on your phone and talk to you out loud, with their own voice. You can also ring them. While you talk you can approve what they are waiting for. They never call in the hours you blocked.

## What to ask for
- "Call me when the build is done." Pinchy rings your phone when it is, and tells you how it went.
- "/call" in the chat, or the call button on an agent: your phone rings now and Pinchy (or that agent) is on the line.
- On the phone, open Pinchy or an agent and press the call button to ring them yourself.
- "Don't call me at night" or "no calls this Saturday". He sets your do-not-call hours.

## On the call screen
- **Answer** (blue) or **Decline** (red).
- **Not now**: no call now and no call back; anything it needed stays in your normal list of things waiting for you.
- **Call me in 15 minutes**: it rings again in 15 minutes (5 to 120 can be set), inside the hours you allow.
- In the middle you see who is calling: Pinchy, the agent or the krill, as you know them.
- **Talk over him**: start speaking and he stops at once to listen. A breath, a cough or a car going by does not stop him: he waits for real words.
- **Approvals**: what the caller needs your OK for is shown with who asks, what it is and what it is for. Tap Approve or Deny. For small everyday things a clear "yes" or "no" out loud is enough. Anything bigger (payments, passwords, commands on your computer, anything that needs your fingerprint) only takes the button or the fingerprint, never your voice. A question like "what is it for?" is never taken as a yes.
- While he thinks after you finish talking, the screen shows it; his answer starts playing while the rest is still being said.

## Settings
- Settings → Pinchy → Calls: Pinchy may call my phone (on/off), who may call (Pinchy, agents, krill; krill are off at first), quiet hours (days and times, over midnight is fine, or all day), specific dates (all day or from–to), whether urgent calls may ring anyway.
- "Ring me for urgent asks when I'm away from the PC" (off at first): when something waits for your OK, you are not at the computer, and the decision model is sure it is urgent, Pinchy rings you with it on the call screen.
- The same do-not-call sheet is in the phone app.

## Limits
- A call needs the Pinchy app on a paired phone. Without one, Pinchy sends a normal message instead.
- One call at a time. A second caller sends a message, or rings again later.
- Calls go through your computer: agents on your away server can call while the computer is on; with it off, they send a normal message.
- Your words are turned into text on your computer (the dictation model); the voice also runs on your computer.
