# Spaces: where things get made

A space keeps one piece of work together: how it should be done, the files it needs, and everything made in it. Documents, sheets, slides, charts, drawings and web pages live there as items. Every item stays yours to edit like a document, Pinchy and krill can change it too, and every version is kept.

Spaces replaced Artifacts and Projects. Your old projects are spaces now with their files and notes, your old artifacts are items in My space, and a copy of both was backed up before they moved.

## What to ask for
- "Make a report on our Q3 numbers." A document item opens in Spaces.
- "Turn that into a 10 slide deck." Slides you can move around, present and export.
- "Make me a small page with a five-minute timer." A web page item that runs right there.
- "Make a space for my tax return." A space. Add its instructions and files, and everything done in it reads them.
- "Change the second slide's title to Friday." He changes that block and leaves the rest alone.
- "Export the deck as PowerPoint." A .pptx file.

## How it works
- Open Spaces from the right-click menu. The start page asks what you are making: describe it and a krill makes it in a space, start blank, or start from a template.
- My space is where anything without a space lands.
- A space's page shows what was made there, what is working there now, its instructions, its knowledge files and its notes. Everything done in the space reads the instructions, files and notes.
- Click an item to edit it. Documents work like a word processor, sheets like a spreadsheet with formulas, slides and drawings by dragging things around. Web pages and code are source you can type in; a page runs beside its source. Pictures have their own editor.
- Slides and drawings: pick several with Shift to align them, space them evenly or group them. Each slide has transitions, including Morph: duplicate a slide, move things on the copy, and set Morph on it. Pick something to give it an animation and the click it comes in on; the numbers on the slide show the order. Layers lists what is on the slide, front first, and hides things when presenting. Every slide holds a theme, a master colour and slide numbers, and Put on every slide moves something onto all of them. Crop a picture by dragging on it, Reset crop brings the rest back. Right click things and thumbnails for more.
- Present shows the deck, a click at a time. Presenter also shows the next slide, your notes and a timer, in one window. Escape ends both.
- A picture has crop, turn, flip, light, contrast, colour, black and white and a width to resize to. Saving makes a new version and keeps the first.
- Ask, beside an item, starts a krill on that item. It changes the blocks it needs. If you are typing in a block, its change waits there as a suggestion you accept or dismiss, so it never writes over you.
- Versions, beside an item, lists every version and who made it. Bring one back and it becomes a new version, so nothing is lost going back.
- Right click a card or a space in the list: open, rename, duplicate, move to another space, export, or move to the trash. Delete on a selected card asks and then moves it to the trash too.
- Right click anything inside an item too: a slide element, a chart, a table, a picture, a block of text or a cell. Delete, duplicate, cut, copy, paste, move and edit are there, plus what fits that kind, and the Delete key works on what's picked.
- Documents have underline, alignment, find and replace (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+H), table looks, sorting a table and comments on a block. Sheets have cell formats, Autosum, filters, find and replace, function hints and comments on a cell. Charts have bars, Stacked, Donut, Radar, axis titles, a legend, numbers on the chart and a colour per series.
- Files, beside an item: add, drop or paste files on that item. Attach files to what you describe on the home page or a space's page, and the krill gets them. Everything in the space can read a space's files.
- Code and data items have line numbers, find and replace, wrap and a language. A web page item shows source, page or both, at desktop, tablet or phone width, and Picture puts one in at the cursor.
- A video has speed, stepping, a caption, and a start and end point that set which part plays. The file itself stays whole.
- New → Import a file turns a Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, CSV or HTML file into an editable item.
- Pinchy, agents and krill can do what you can, on every kind: find and replace, tables, cells and formulas, sort and filter, cell styles, comments, chart options, slide transitions and animations, groups, align, crop, turn, trim. They use one tool, space_edit, and do:"verbs" lists what an item can do.
- Export saves an item as a file: docx, xlsx, pptx, pdf, html, png, svg, webm or mp4, depending on the kind. A pptx keeps what it can and says what it can't: Rise, Drop, Zoom and Spin play as Fade, transition times become slow, medium or fast, Morph needs PowerPoint 2019 or later, groups aren't PowerPoint groups, hidden things are left out and a master is copied onto each slide. Comments reach no export.
- People and sharing are on the People tab beside an item; see the Lobster Network page.

## Settings
- Settings → Safety & data → Backup & move: spaces are part of a backup.
- Settings → Agents & automation → Away server: spaces sync with your away server.

## Limits
- One version of an item can be up to 8 MB, a picture or video up to 100 MB, a space up to 1 GB. Bigger files go in Cloud storage.
- A space holds up to 2000 items, 500 files and 1 GB of files, one file up to 100 MB. An item keeps its first version and its latest 199.
- A web page runs in a sandbox: all its code inside it, no internet and no access to your files.
- PDF, PNG and video exports need the desktop app. On the away server those formats say so and the others work.
- A removed item stays in its space's trash for 30 days. A removed space stays in the trash.
