# New in Pinchy 2.8

This page lists what 2.8 added and which words start each thing. Every line points to the page with the details; ask Pinchy in your own words and he picks the right tool.

## What to ask for
- "Ask Scout which figures in the report are final." Pinchy, agents and krill can now write to each other and wait for the answer, with files. See "Pinchy, agents and krill talk to each other".
- "Call me when the build is done." Pinchy, your agents and krill can ring the Pinchy app on your phone and talk to you in their own voice; you can ring them too (/call, or Call on an agent). Not now / Call me in 15 minutes, and do-not-call hours in Settings → Pinchy → Calls. See the Calls page.
- "Split this into parts and do them at the same time." A krill can start its own krill for parts that stand alone, and Pinchy can say first what a job will cost. See the Krill page.
- "Find the three cheapest Raspberry Pi 5 offers and give me a table." Research on the web reads many pages at once and can pull a clean table out of them; a captcha or a login step comes to you. See the Browser page.
- "Put that in a document for me." Reports, tables and plans go straight into the Reef as a document you can edit, not a loose file. See the Reef page.
- "Open the settings in that app and turn on dark mode." On your desktop he now reads an app's buttons and fields by name first, so he clicks the right thing; he reads text in pictures too. See "Using your computer".
- "Answer me out loud." The Humotranslator: Pinchy talks while he writes, reads the whole answer, in the voice you pick (British, Scottish, Narrator, Sea captain… or one you describe), and every agent and krill has its own voice and moods. See the Voice page.
- "Sign me up for this newsletter." He fills a form with what is in your vault without seeing it; to save a new password you press Save yourself. See the Vault page.
- "What did my agents change today?" and "undo that." The timeline shows every step your helpers took, and undoes one. See "Undo, pause and permissions".
- "Show me what my agents are doing." The aquarium in the Reef shows them at work. See the Reef page.
- "Cut the first ten seconds of my video and add a title." A Video item in the Reef takes your clips and edits them. See the Reef page.
- "Design a small board with a USB-C socket and an LED, and a case to 3D print for it." A CAD item in the Reef is a part to print (STL/3MF, STEP) or a circuit board to order (one zip for PCBWay or JLCPCB, with an assembly guide). See "CAD: parts to print and boards to order".
- "Fix the typo in my Monday notes", from your phone. The Pinchy app has the Reef as its own tab: open any document, sheet, chart or slides and change it right there, tap Krill on any item to have it changed for you, make new ones, send a video from your phone as a Video clip, and watch the Aquarium (the same 3D creatures as the desktop). Update the app to get it. See the Phone page.
- "Tell me when the build log says it failed." A watch can now read the meaning of text with the small decision model on your computer, for free, and fires only when it is sure twice; it also tells you once when a krill is stuck or waiting. See "The decision model".
- "Wait until the page has loaded, then click Save." Pinchy waits for a screen to really finish loading instead of guessing a pause. See "Using your computer" and "The decision model".
- "@Scout, please add the source here": type @ in a Reef document (or a comment) and pick an agent, Pinchy or a krill; they see it in their next run and answer in the document. See "What your agents and krill hand you".
- "Keep this document up to date every morning." The Keep updated button on a document (or ask Pinchy) has a krill re-check it on a schedule and change only what went out of date. See "What your agents and krill hand you".
- "Switch to Liquid Glass with Tokyo Night Moon." Styles change how Pinchy is built (corners, fonts, depth, real glass on the floating pieces) and never your colours: any style goes with any colour theme. Type **/style** or see "Look, themes and tricks".
- "Use my API key for this other site." A login, card or key now works on any site, not only the one it was saved for: the card says what it is, what it's for, where it goes and who asks; a site that isn't its own needs your passphrase or your phone. See the Vault page.
- "Where did my pocket money go?" Pinchy can't pay for things any more. If an older version had a wallet with crypto in it, in Settings → Wallet → Old wallet, you send it to an address of yours. See "Start here: what Pinchy is for and what to ask him".
- On a call, say yes to small things; anything bigger asks for the button or your fingerprint. See the Calls page.
- Type **/goal** and what should be true ("/goal all the tests pass"): he keeps going until it is. See "Chat commands".

## For people who build on Pinchy
- Every engine (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, opencode and the others, and his own native one) now sees the same tools. One that can't load all of them gets two doors: one to find a tool by plain words, one to run it. A long loop over many tools can go in one small script.
- Before he says a change is done, he checks it really happened.
- Coding tools that run as krill (Claude Code, Cursor and the other command-line ones) work in a box on Linux: they can't read Pinchy's private folders (his keys, his vault, other sessions' passes).

## Settings
Nothing to set on this page: each feature's own page names its settings.
